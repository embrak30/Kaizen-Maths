# AP Calculus worksheet feasibility audit

111 topics: 2 pilots, 36 reusable procedural cores, 73 new or substantially adapted AP packs. 17 generators / 195 subtypes / 390 sample outputs checked for adapter operation, not mathematical correctness.

All topics are feasible candidates for fixed original PDFs. No claim that packs are already authored or ready to publish. Static packs do not require interactive generators first.
