Limits and First Principles

Overview

This calculus tool develops limits of functions and first-principles differentiation. It is designed for careful notation, explicit substitution, cancellation, rationalising, one-sided limits, and derivative definitions.

Level Guide

  • Level 1: Direct limits, factorising before substitution, rationalising surd limits, and limit laws.
  • Level 2: One-sided limits, piecewise functions, numerical tables, and limits as x tends to infinity.
  • Level 3: Differentiation from first principles for linear, quadratic, cubic, and point-gradient cases.
  • Level 4: Continuity, parameter limits, tangent equations from first principles, and mixed limit practice.

Teaching Focus

  • Keep the limit notation in place until the expression is safe to evaluate.
  • Do not substitute into a fraction while the denominator is zero; simplify first.
  • For first principles, expand f(x+h), subtract f(x), factor h, cancel h, then take the limit as h tends to zero.
