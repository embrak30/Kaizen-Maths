Polygons and Angles Guidance

Overview

This tool practises interior and exterior angle facts for regular and irregular polygons, including algebraic angle problems.

Core Facts

  • The sum of the interior angles of an n-sided polygon is (n - 2) times 180 degrees.
  • The exterior angles of any convex polygon add to 360 degrees.
  • In a regular polygon, all interior angles are equal and all exterior angles are equal.
  • For a regular polygon, each exterior angle is 360 degrees divided by n.
  • Interior angle + exterior angle = 180 degrees at each vertex.

Teaching Focus

  • Separate the two big ideas: interior angle sum and exterior angle sum.
  • Ask students to identify whether the polygon is regular before dividing by the number of sides.
  • For algebra problems, insist on writing the angle-sum equation before simplifying.
  • Use the diagram to identify which angle is interior and which angle is exterior.
