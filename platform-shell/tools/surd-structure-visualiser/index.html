Surd Structure Visualiser

Purpose

This tool shows simplification of surds as a square area and side-length argument.

Teaching Move

  • Begin with the area of the whole square, not with the shortcut.
  • Ask pupils what the side length must be if the area is known.
  • Use the square factor to split the whole square into equal smaller squares.
  • Connect the side count to the coefficient outside the radical.

Key Point

The coefficient is not moved outside the radical by magic. It is the number of equal radical side lengths along one side of the original square.
