Midpoint and Length Guidance

Overview

This tool supports coordinate geometry practice for midpoint, length of a line segment, missing endpoints, and combined challenge questions.

Level Guide

  • Level 1: midpoint formula and finding an endpoint from a midpoint.
  • Level 2: horizontal, vertical, and diagonal line segment lengths.
  • Level 3: missing coordinates using midpoint or distance information.
  • Level 4: combined midpoint and length problems, plus circle diameter links.

Teaching Focus

  • Midpoint means average the x-coordinates and average the y-coordinates separately.
  • Length comes from horizontal and vertical changes, then Pythagoras.
  • For missing endpoints, use the fact that the midpoint sits exactly halfway between the endpoints.
