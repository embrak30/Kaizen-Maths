TEACHER INSTRUCTIONS
Teacher Instructions

Purpose

This tool focuses on factorising quadratic expressions. It separates recognition practice from harder complete factorisation so pupils can see the structure before mixing methods.

Teaching Flow

  • Start with monic quadratics and the product-sum relationship.
  • Move to coefficient-of-x-squared questions using split middle term and grouping.
  • Insist on taking out a common factor first where one exists.
  • Check answers by expanding the brackets.

Common Misconceptions

  • Finding two numbers that multiply correctly but do not add to the middle coefficient.
  • Forgetting to split the middle term when the coefficient of x squared is not 1.
  • Stopping after taking out the common factor rather than factorising the quadratic fully.
  • Writing brackets that expand to the wrong sign pattern.
