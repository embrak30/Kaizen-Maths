Decimal Place Value

Purpose

This tool develops place-value understanding from whole-number columns into tenths, hundredths, and thousandths.

Teaching Emphasis

  • Use column language: tens, ones, tenths, hundredths, thousandths.
  • Connect each decimal column to a fraction of one whole.
  • When multiplying or dividing by powers of 10, say that the digits move through fixed place-value columns. The decimal point stays fixed between ones and tenths.

Common Misconceptions

  • Thinking 0.08 is larger than 0.7 because 8 is larger than 7.
  • Reading 3.45 as “three point forty-five” without recognising tenths and hundredths.
  • Moving the decimal point instead of moving digits into new place-value columns.
