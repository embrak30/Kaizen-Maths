Teacher guidance

Topic practice gives four questions. Linked tasks connect calculations within one situation. Grades indicate teaching progression. Grade 4 focuses on fractions, money and measurement; Grades 5–6 add percentages, ratio and reverse reasoning. Choose practice to match what your class has covered.

Suggested marks reward correct methods as well as answers. Accept equivalent methods and sensible diagrams. Carry a pupil’s earlier numerical error forward when the next method is correct. These are suggested classroom marks. Use the Questions view for pupils; the other views reveal answers. Assessment-style is the default: no added solution scaffolds, with shape diagrams retained where they are part of the question. Switch to With bar-model support to teach the same questions; the wording, values, answers and suggested marks stay the same. Bars are optional Kaizen teaching support. The marks describe the underlying mathematics, not an independent score once support is provided. Print both question versions for professional development. Printing and switching versions do not generate a new set.
