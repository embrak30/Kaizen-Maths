Equation Balance Models

Purpose

This tool helps teachers model equation solving as keeping two sides balanced, before pupils move to abstract algebraic steps.

Teaching Flow

  • Begin with the concrete picture balance so pupils see equal value.
  • Move to algebra tiles and counters once the balance idea is secure.
  • Use the crossed-out objects to show that the same quantity is removed from both sides.
  • For negative terms, distinguish positive counters, negative counters, positive x tiles, and negative x tiles.

Key Questions

  • What must be removed from both sides to keep the balance true?
  • What is left after the removal?
  • How many equal x groups are left?
  • What does one group represent?
