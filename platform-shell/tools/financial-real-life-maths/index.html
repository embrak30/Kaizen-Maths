Financial Real-Life Maths

Overview

This tool practises practical financial mathematics for GCSE, IGCSE, CSEC, and functional real-life number work.

Level Guide

  • Level 1: best buys, discounts, VAT, and exchange rates.
  • Level 2: wages, overtime, commission, profit, markup, and margin.
  • Level 3: simple interest, compound interest, depreciation, loans, and hire purchase.
  • Level 4: mixed finance decisions, reverse calculations, and budget problems.

Teaching Focus

  • Identify the original amount before applying a percentage change.
  • Keep money rounded to two decimal places, but avoid rounding too early in a multi-step calculation.
  • Separate percentage markup from profit margin: they use different denominators.
  • When comparing options, calculate the total cost of each option before deciding.

Common Misconceptions

  • Adding successive percentage changes instead of applying them in order.
  • Applying VAT to the original price when the question says a discount happens first.
  • Dividing by the wrong exchange rate when converting currencies.
  • Confusing interest earned with the final amount in a savings or loan context.
