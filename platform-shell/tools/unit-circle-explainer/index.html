Teaching with the unit circle

Move through Circle, Coordinates, Signs, Radians & exact values, Graphs, and Have a go. Drag the point or use the degree controls. Arrow keys turn through 1°; Shift + arrows turn through 15°. Home selects 0° and End selects 360°. The controls cover −360° to 720°.

The radius is 1, so P = (cos θ, sin θ). These are signed coordinates: right/left gives the sign of cosine, and up/down gives the sign of sine. Reference-triangle lengths are |cos θ| and |sin θ|.

Tangent is sin θ divided by cos θ. At 90° + 180°k, cosine is zero and tangent is undefined. The optional tangent display uses the full line through O and P and the tangent x = 1. When cosine is negative, the intersection lies on the opposite ray. An off-scale arrow does not mark the actual intersection.

Positive rotation is counterclockwise. For a rotation of |θ| radians on a circle of radius 1, the travelled arc length is |θ|. Negative angles describe clockwise rotation; they do not mean a negative physical distance.

Exact values are supplied at standard angles. Other values are rounded. The graph view shows one cycle from 0 to 2π; angles outside that interval use a coterminal position. Sine and cosine have period 2π; tangent has period π. Tangent curves are not joined across their asymptotes.

The quiz locks the angle and hides values until you reveal the reasoning. Leave Have a go to explore freely again.
