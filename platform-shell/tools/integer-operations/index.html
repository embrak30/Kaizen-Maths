Teacher Instructions

Getting Started

This tool develops directed-number fluency from comparing integers through all four operations and simple contexts.

Level Structure

  • Level 1: Compare, order, and identify opposites on the integer number line.
  • Level 2: Add and subtract integers, including subtracting negative numbers.
  • Level 3: Multiply, divide, and use order of operations with integers.
  • Level 4: Apply integer operations to temperature, money, elevation, and change contexts.

Teaching Focus

  • Keep the language of direction clear: positive means move right or up, negative means move left or down.
  • For subtraction, explicitly change to adding the opposite before calculating.
  • For multiplication and division, separate the size of the answer from the sign of the answer.

Common Mistakes

  • Thinking a negative number with a larger digit is greater because the digit is larger.
  • Treating subtraction of a negative as subtraction again instead of adding the opposite.
  • Applying sign rules for multiplication to addition, where the reasoning is different.
