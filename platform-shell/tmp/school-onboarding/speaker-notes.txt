SLIDE 1
About 2 minutes. Welcome the teachers and leadership team. Today has two purposes: become comfortable using Kaizen and agree how Make It Count will support classroom teaching. School access is already arranged. Do not show or read passwords or access codes on the shared screen. Ask: Which part of lesson preparation takes most of your time? Keep one or two responses to revisit at the end. Presenter identity and programme context supplied by Dr Megel Barker in this conversation.

SLIDE 2
About 2 minutes. Open Explore when you move to the live site. Explain the three destinations using their exact navigation labels. Ready-made sheets save the teacher from choosing settings. The Worksheet Builder provides more questions, mixed topics and customisation. Teaching tools include generators and conceptual explainers. Ask teachers which destination they are most likely to use first. Source: the existing Kaizen application navigation and the resource workflow agreed in this conversation. https://www.kaizenmaths.com/#/tools

SLIDE 3
About 10 minutes, including teacher practice. Switch to your browser at https://www.kaizenmaths.com/#/tools. Keep this slide as your checklist. 1. Teaching Tools: choose a familiar school topic such as fractions, percentages or ratio. Generate a small set and show an answer with its worked solution. 2. Ready-Made Resources: choose a relevant collection and open a Student Pack, then its Teacher Pack. Keep existing print assets ready in case the connection fails. 3. If time allows, open Build a Worksheet, add a question block, show the answers/worked-solution controls and print preview. Then give teachers two minutes to sign in with their arranged access and find a resource for the coming week. Confirm access individually without projecting credentials. Screenshot source: output/resource-navigation-preview.png, a real site screenshot in this repository. It illustrates the workspace navigation and is not a live browser view.

SLIDE 4
About 2 minutes. Explain that Make It Count supports how teachers teach mathematics, alongside their use of resources. Bar modelling is the initial focus. Ask: If three quarters of a quantity is 18, how could a bar help us find the whole? Invite a teacher to describe the three equal parts, one part and the complete quantity. The answer is 24. Emphasise the reference whole, equal parts and the relationships between quantities. Teachers can study worked examples, attempt challenges and discuss explanations. This is professional learning support, not a promise of a formal qualification. Sources: the bar-modelling pilot course in assets/courses and the programme aims supplied in this conversation. Programme artwork: assets/brand/make-it-count-brighter-futures.png.

SLIDE 5
About 2 minutes. Present this as a proposed structure, not a confirmed calendar. Confirm the school’s priorities, availability and participating teachers. Access/onboarding can begin before professional-development sessions. Initial and final reflections can help us understand changes in confidence and practice, but do not establish that the programme caused any changes. Ask for a short anonymous classroom example or teacher reflection, without pupil names. Agree any research participation, consent and use of findings separately before collecting data for research. Do not present routine course participation as consent to a research study. The six-week intention and reflective approach come from the programme discussion in this conversation.

SLIDE 6
About 2 minutes. Ask teachers to name one resource or strategy they will use before the next meeting. Check that everyone has working access. Confirm the named school coordinator, participating teachers, the next session date and the agreed contact channel. Write these in your meeting notes rather than inventing them in advance. Close by returning to the planning need teachers named at the beginning. Keep a few minutes for questions if the demonstration finishes early. No session dates, school identity, access credentials or contact details have been assumed.
