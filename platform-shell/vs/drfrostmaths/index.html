A practical comparison

Kaizen Maths or Dr Frost Maths: which fits your teaching?

Start with the work you need to do: prepare and explain maths, or manage a wider pupil homework and progress workflow.

What Dr Frost Maths does well

Dr Frost brings lesson resources, exam questions, homework and pupil progress tracking together. Its published UK school offer is £650 + VAT per school per year, including all teacher and student accounts, with a 60-day trial.

Where Kaizen Maths differs

Kaizen Maths focuses on teacher-led question generation, revealable worked steps, board annotation and printable worksheets, starters and exit tickets. Its coverage map includes GCSE, CSEC, CAPE and Oman GED alongside other routes. Adults sign in; enabled pupil tasks use teacher-issued codes and aliases rather than pupil accounts.

Make It Count adds a proposed resource-plus-development route. It is subject to funding and agreement, not training bundled with an individual subscription.

Compare the account scope as well as the price

Kaizen Maths teacher access is £9.99/month or £89/year after a 30-day trial. School access starts at £299/year for 1–5 teachers; this is not the same account scope as an unlimited-school licence.

Dr Frost may be the better fit if whole-school pupil accounts and integrated homework tracking are your priority. Try Kaizen Maths if your immediate job is fresh board practice and printable maths across several curriculum routes.

Neither option should be selected on price alone: test a topic against your scheme of work.

Sources checked 27 October 2026: Dr Frost features, Dr Frost pricing, Mathletics UK. Offers and coverage can change; check each provider before buying.
