Our story

Maths resources shaped by the work of teaching.

Kaizen Maths is developed by Dr Megel Barker, who has over 30 years of experience in mathematics teaching, curriculum development and leadership.

One place for the resources a maths teacher needs

Working in different countries and across different curricula, Megel saw the same practical need: teachers should be able to find the resources they need for mathematics in one place, connected to the teaching they are doing.

Kaizen Maths brings unlimited question generation, worksheets and test creation together with the classroom tools used to explain the mathematics. Teachers can move from an idea to an example, from an example to practice, and from practice to an assessment without rebuilding the lesson across several websites.

Practice with a purpose

At the heart of Kaizen is continuous improvement. Megel believes that the chance to practise, revisit a method and try fresh questions gives learners more opportunities to work towards mastery.

Unlimited questions are part of that approach. So are worksheets and assessments that teachers can create around the topic, level and support their pupils need.

Understanding the mathematics, not just following the steps

Kaizen also includes conceptual teaching tools that help teachers make mathematical ideas visible and explore why a method works. These sit alongside the practice resources, so understanding and fluency can develop together.

The opportunity to learn matters for teachers too. Megel wants teachers to be able to explore the theory behind a concept and its development, strengthening their own understanding as they prepare to explain it to pupils.

Supporting teachers and pupils

The aim is to make mathematics teaching easier: easier access to resources, clearer explanations and less time spent searching and formatting. Where the pupil module is enabled, pupils can also access teacher-set tasks using the school's or tutor's task codes and aliases.

Megel works with his team at Embark Education to continue developing Kaizen Maths around those needs. The resource keeps growing, with the day-to-day work of mathematics teaching at its centre.
