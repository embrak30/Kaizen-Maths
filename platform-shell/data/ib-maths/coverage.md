## Boundaries and further depth

All five mathematical areas are represented in each course. This is a substantial topic-practice collection, not a claim that every syllabus bullet or assessment variation is exhausted. Further dedicated banks can extend: nonlinear regression diagnostics and fitted-model comparison; formal vector-line/plane intersections and angle cases; general matrix diagonalisation; normal approximations and variance combinations; further mixed Maclaurin functions and proofs; richer three-site Voronoi changes; and longer multi-topic investigation questions.

The mathematical exploration, full examination papers, grade boundaries, official marking conventions and the 2029 revision are outside this collection. Use the current school guide alongside this library when planning complete syllabus coverage.
