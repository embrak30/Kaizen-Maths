## Use and boundaries

The collection provides quick topic practice across all three modules. It does not claim to exhaust every syllabus objective or examination variation. Full mock papers, SBA projects, grading thresholds and official CXC marking schemes are separate resources. Longer contextual investigations, multi-topic assessment and additional variants can be developed independently.

All tax, tariff, insurance and exchange-rate examples state illustrative rules within the question. Existing general practice may use other currencies; the arithmetic does not depend on current rates. Standard deviation is interpreted without requiring its calculation; the new histogram bank uses equal class widths. Existing teaching tools may contain material beyond CSEC.
